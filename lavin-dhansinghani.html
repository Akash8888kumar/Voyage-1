Co-Founder & President, Business Development

Lavin Dhansinghani

Lavin Dhansinghani is a seasoned travel industry professional with over 18 years of experience in destination management, global business development, and strategic partnerships. As Co-Founder & Director of Voyage 1, he has played a pivotal role in building the company’s international footprint and establishing trusted relationships across the global travel ecosystem.

His expertise spans destination management, tourism representation, global hotel and supplier contracting, MICE, FIT and group travel, with a strong focus on creating sustainable business opportunities for travel partners worldwide. Over the years, he has cultivated enduring partnerships with tourism boards, hotels, airlines, and travel trade professionals across multiple international destinations.

Driven by a vision to connect global destinations with the travel trade through trusted partnerships and local expertise, Lavin continues to lead Voyage 1’s strategic growth. His commitment to innovation, collaboration, and service excellence has helped position Voyage 1 as a reliable global destination management partner.
